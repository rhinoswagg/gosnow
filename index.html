FAQ

Common Questions

Quick answers to what people ask us most. Have more questions? Just call or text!

How much does it cost to rent the shave ice machine?+

Our base rental starts at $150/hour and includes the machine, syrups, and up to 100 servings. Add-on packages are available for cups, ice, supplies, and an attendant. Contact us for a custom quote for your event.

What does the base rental include?+

The base rental includes the shave ice machine, syrups, and up to 100 servings. We'll show you how to operate the machine at setup. A separate supplies package covers cups, spoons, straws, and ice.

Do you deliver the machine to my event?+

Yes! Delivery is available for an additional fee based on your event location. Pickup is also an option if you prefer to bring the machine yourself. Contact us to confirm delivery availability for your area.

What areas do you serve?+

GoSnow is based in Granbury, Texas and serves the surrounding areas including Hood County, Glen Rose, Weatherford, and nearby communities. Contact us to confirm availability for your specific event location.

How far in advance should I book?+

We recommend booking as early as possible, especially for weekends and summer events. That said, reach out even with short notice — we'll do our best to make it work!
