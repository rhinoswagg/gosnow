Pricing & Booking

Pricing & Booking Questions

How much does it cost to rent the shave ice machine?+

The base rental starts at $150/hour and includes the shave ice machine, syrups, and up to 100 servings. Optional add-ons like a supplies package (cups, ice, straws) and an attendant/operator are available for an additional fee. Contact us for a custom quote based on your event details.

Is the price per hour or a flat rate?+

The base rental is priced per hour. If you need a longer event or a flat-rate arrangement, just ask — we're happy to discuss options that work for your event.

How do I book GoSnow for my event?+

The easiest way is to call or text us at 734-406-4688, or fill out the quote request form on our Contact page. Tell us your event date, location, and approximate guest count, and we'll confirm availability and get you a quote.

How far in advance should I book?+

We recommend booking as early as possible — especially for weekends and summer events, which fill up fast. That said, reach out even on short notice and we'll do our best to make it work!

Is a deposit required?+

Deposit and payment details will be confirmed at the time of booking. Contact us and we'll walk you through everything.

What is your cancellation policy?+

Cancellation terms will be provided when you book. We understand that plans change — reach out as soon as possible if something comes up and we'll work with you.

What's Included

What Comes with the Rental

What does the base rental include?+

The base rental includes the shave ice machine, syrups and flavors, and up to 100 servings. We'll also walk you through how to use the machine at setup. Cups, ice, straws, and spoons are available as a separate add-on package.

Are cups, ice, and straws included?+

Cups, ice, straws, and spoons are available as a separate supplies add-on package — they are not included in the base rental. Ask about adding the supplies package when you request a quote.

What if I need more than 100 servings?+

No problem — just let us know your estimated guest count when you reach out. We'll make sure you have enough syrup and supplies to cover everyone.

Do you provide an attendant to operate the machine?+

With the base rental, we'll show you how to use the machine and you'll operate it yourself. If you'd prefer a GoSnow team member to stay on-site and run the machine throughout your event, an attendant service is available for an additional fee. Contact us to add this to your quote.

Do I need to provide power, water, or a table?+

Power and setup requirements will be confirmed when you book — we'll make sure you know exactly what you need before your event. Contact us for specifics.

Delivery & Pickup

Delivery & Logistics

Do you deliver the machine to my event?+

Yes! Delivery is available for an additional fee based on your event location. We'll bring the machine to your venue and get everything set up. Contact us to confirm delivery availability for your area and get a delivery quote.

Can I pick up the machine myself?+

Absolutely. Pickup is available if you prefer to transport the machine yourself. We'll have it ready and walk you through everything before you head out.

What areas do you serve?+

GoSnow is based in Granbury, Texas and primarily serves Hood County and surrounding areas including Glen Rose, Weatherford, Tolar, Cresson, Acton, and nearby communities. Contact us to confirm availability for your specific event location.

Flavors

Flavors & Options

What flavors are available?+

GoSnow offers a variety of bold, colorful shave ice flavors. Examples include blue raspberry, cherry, strawberry, watermelon, mango, grape, green apple, tiger's blood, lemon, pineapple, peach, and coconut. Contact us to confirm which flavors are available for your event date.

Can I request specific flavors?+

We'll do our best to accommodate flavor requests. Reach out when you book and let us know if you have a preference — we'll confirm what we can include.

Are the flavors included in the base rental?+

Events

Events & General Questions

What types of events do you serve?+

GoSnow serves all kinds of events — birthday parties, school field days, church gatherings, youth group events, sports team parties, corporate events, fundraisers, weddings, community festivals, block parties, and more. If people are getting together, we can probably be there.

Is shave ice good for large events?+

Yes! A shave ice machine serves guests quickly and efficiently. The base rental includes up to 100 servings per hour. For larger events, contact us and we'll make sure you have the right setup to keep everyone happy.

What if the weather is bad on my event day?+

Weather policy details will be confirmed at the time of booking. Contact us if weather is a concern — we'll talk through your options.

Is shave ice messy?+

Shave ice is generally a clean, easy treat to serve. Having cups and straws helps keep things tidy. We'll give you tips at setup to make the experience smooth for your guests.
