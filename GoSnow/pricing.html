Pricing Questions

Frequently Asked Pricing Questions

Is the price per hour or flat rate?+

The base rental is priced per hour at $150/hr. This includes the machine, syrups, and up to 100 servings. Contact us to discuss flat-rate options for longer events.

What if I need more than 100 servings?+

For larger events, just let us know your guest count when you request a quote. We'll make sure you have enough syrup and supplies for everyone.

Do I need to put down a deposit?+

Deposit and payment details will be confirmed when you book. Contact us and we'll walk you through the booking process.

What is your cancellation policy?+

Cancellation terms will be provided at time of booking. Contact us directly for details — we'll work with you if something comes up.

Do you charge a delivery fee?+

Yes, delivery is available for an additional fee based on your event location. Pickup is free. Contact us with your address and we'll give you an exact quote.
